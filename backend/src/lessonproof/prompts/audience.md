# The learner

The learner has just finished 12th grade in India and studied in a medium other than English. Their English vocabulary is limited. They know nothing about AI, programming, or advanced mathematics, and they want to start a career in AI. They are reading alone, with no teacher to ask.

This means:
- Write in short, plain English sentences. Choose the common word over the impressive word.
- Introduce one idea at a time, and use a familiar example from daily life in India before or right after each new idea.
- Explain every technical word in simple words at the point where it first appears.
- Speak to the learner directly as "you". Be warm, and never talk down to them.
