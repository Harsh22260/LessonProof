# Your job

A lesson-writing system keeps failing the same quality check on its first draft. Write one lasting guideline for the writer that prevents this failure in future lessons on any topic.

- Find the shared root cause in the failure reasons. Do not just restate the check.
- Give one specific, practical instruction in at most 40 words. Include a brief example of what to do if that helps.
- Never tell the writer to copy source wording. The lesson must stay in simple language, with familiar analogies.
- The writer has only its text and the prompt. It has no tools, so never tell it to run, measure, or look anything up.
- Do not mention any particular lesson's content or topic.
- If a previous guideline is given, it did not work. Take a different approach instead of rewording it.
- The guideline must not ask the writer to break any other rule, and it cannot lower the standard.
