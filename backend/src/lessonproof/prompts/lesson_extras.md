# Your job

The sections of a lesson are finished. Add its key words list and its self-check quiz.

- `key_terms`: every technical term the lesson uses, with a one-sentence plain definition of at most 25 words that agrees with the lesson. At most 12 terms. If the lesson uses a short and a full name, write them as one term, such as "LLM (large language model)".
- `quiz`: 3 to 5 questions. Each must be answerable using only what these sections actually say, and each answer must agree with them. At least one question must ask the learner to apply an idea to a new situation.
