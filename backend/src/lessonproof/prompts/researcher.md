# Your job

Prepare the reference sheet that a lesson on the given topic will be written from and checked against.

This reference is for a complete beginner who finished 12th grade. Keep to the basic ideas of the topic. Leave out advanced mathematics, formulas, and specialist terms, even if they are correct.

- `facts`: 10 to 18 short, specific, well-established facts, written in simple language. Use ids F1, F2, and so on. Leave out anything you are unsure about. Do not invent sources: fill in `source` only with a real reference you are confident of, otherwise leave it empty.
- `key_points`: the 5 to 7 ideas a beginner must understand, covering what the topic is, why it matters, and how it works. State general ideas, and do not require any specific example or list of examples. Use ids K1, K2, and so on.
- `must_define`: at most 8 technical terms the lesson will need.
- `glossary`: for each of those terms, a one-sentence plain definition.
- `misconceptions`: common false beliefs about the topic.
- `aliases`: other names for the topic, in lowercase.
